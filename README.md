# MarketLens

> AI-Powered Market Intelligence & Competitive Analytics Platform


## Overview

MarketLens is an enterprise-grade AI platform that transforms scattered market data into actionable business intelligence.

The platform continuously analyzes public information from multiple sources—including industry news, competitor websites, customer reviews, hiring trends, and market reports—to uncover emerging trends, monitor competitors, forecast opportunities, and support strategic decision-making.


## Features

- Competitor Intelligence
- Market Trend Detection
- Customer Sentiment Analysis
- Topic Discovery
- Knowledge Graph Exploration
- Trend Forecasting
- AI Executive Reports
- Interactive Analytics Dashboard
